**Free Underground Tekno Radio** is an online radio station based in Italy dedicated to free tekno music, founded by the members of the **Space Invaders Tekno Sound** collective in late 2008. 👽

It streams music 24/7, focusing on tracks from free parties and sound systems. 
The main archive contains 200+ recordings made by hundreds of free tekno artists across decades, all around the world, while performing at the **[WebTek Streaming Teknival](https://github.com/fabriziosalmi/webtek/blob/master/README.md)** editions. 

_If you want to know more about the WebTek, click [**here**](https://github.com/fabriziosalmi/webtek)_
